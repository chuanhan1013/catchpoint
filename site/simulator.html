CatchPoint

CatchPoint is a predictive, delay-compensated safety layer that lets a patient attempt a recovery step and applies only the graded pelvic force needed to keep that step recoverable.

The problem

0.28 s

Reported mechanical response for the MRBA: a reactive system has little time after instability is obvious.

0.8 s

Reported IMU-walker actuation delay: a high threshold can arrive after the useful recovery step.

n = 99

A fixed-dose stroke perturbation study found no benefit over BWS alone, motivating adaptive challenge.

How CatchPoint decides

flowchart LR
  A[Estimate COM + capture point ξ] --> B{ξ inside
  patient's recoverable region?}
  B -- yes --> L0[L0 · TRANSPARENT
  F = 0, patient steps]
  B -- no --> C{Minimum force ≤ 150 N?}
  C -- yes --> L1[L1 · ASSIST
  graded pelvic force]
  C -- no --> L2[L2 · CATCH
  delayed harness lock]
  L0 --> A
  L1 --> A
  L2 --> E[Safe stop]
