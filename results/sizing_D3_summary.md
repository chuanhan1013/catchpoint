Simulation and statics only; unbuilt, unvalidated; assumptions in analysis/i2_sizing/report.md section 2.6.
Generated by analysis/i2_sizing/make_report.py from the JSON data through publish.py (rounding policy: report.md, top): loads, resultants, maxima and upper CI bounds are rounded up; capacities, safety factors, envelopes, minima, coverage percentages and lower CI bounds are rounded down; "median" and "p95" are descriptive statistics (nearest). Do not edit by hand.
Paths below are relative to analysis/i2_sizing/. D3 geometry comes from data/d3_contract.json (also read by cad/build_cad_d3.py; checked by test_d3_consistency.py).
