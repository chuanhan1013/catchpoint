# CatchPoint — role table (concise, 22 Oct)

Talk order: **A → D → B → C → E** (four handovers; E closes). About 16 min + 5 min Q&A.

| # | Role | Slides | Time | Scope (one line) | Delivers | Must answer in Q&A |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **A Clinical** | 2–4 problem, existing solutions, innovation matrix | ~3 min | Why patients need this; what others already do | Problem stats + sources; gap analysis; source for every matrix cell | Where do the numbers come from? Why are current systems not enough? |
| 2 | **D Control** (Chuanhan) | 5–7 three contributions, control architecture & timing, where they must step vs can reach | ~3.5 min | When to act and how much force | Decision flow + timing; minimum-force rule; technical integration and number consistency | How is this different from RobUST / MRBA? How are delays handled? |
| 3 | **B Mechanical** | 8–13 cable interface + front gate, exploded views, tipping, three responses (render + physics video) | ~3 min | Can the hardware nudge, catch and not tip? | D3 CAD; FBDs; tension / brake / tip-margin table | Where does 300 N come from? Why outriggers? |
| 4 | **C Sensing** | 14–18 sensing & estimation, physics patient, physics demo, walking demo (held-out test) | ~3 min | How the robot knows the state; the whole system running | Sensor layout + delay budget; calibration protocol; demo videos | Which sensors? Is the physics demo fair? |
| 5 | **E Simulation** | 19–22 results, limitations, clinical validation plan, summary | ~3.5 min | How strong the evidence is, what is missing, how to validate next | Frozen numbers with denominators and CIs; limitations + validation plan (with A) | How simplified is the model? Is there a held-out test? |

**Shared rules**: quote only the frozen evidence files, with denominators; no prototype or clinical-effect claims; novelty
"to our knowledge"; everyone re-runs or reads their part and can present it without notes.
